Account Deletion Request

If you would like to delete your BMTheaterHub account and associated personal information, you can submit an account deletion request using the information below.

Please send your deletion request from the email address associated with your BMTheaterHub account.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to:

noreply@bmtheaterhub.com

Use the subject: Account Deletion Request

Please include the email address associated with your BMTheaterHub account so that we can identify your account and process your request.

What Happens After Your Request?

  • Your account deletion request will be reviewed.
  • Your BMTheaterHub account and associated personal profile information will be scheduled for deletion.
  • Information that must be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons may be retained where required.

Need Help?

If you have any questions about account deletion, contact the BMTheaterHub support team.
