Privacy Policy

Introduction

BMTheaterHub respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and how we protect it when you use our website or Android application.

Information We Collect

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Artist Profile Information
  • City & State
  • WhatsApp Number
  • Profile Photo
  • Resume (PDF)
  • Performance Video

How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your artist profile.
  • Display your public profile.
  • Provide artist discovery features.
  • Enable communication through WhatsApp.
  • Improve platform performance and user experience.
  • Protect the platform against abuse and fraud.

Media Storage

Profile photos, resumes and performance videos uploaded on BMTheaterHub are securely stored using Cloudinary. These files remain associated with your artist profile until removed or replaced by you.

Data Security

We use industry standard security practices to protect your information. While no online service can guarantee absolute security, we continuously work to safeguard your personal data.

Your Rights

  • Update your profile information.
  • Replace uploaded files.
  • Delete your resume.
  • Delete your performance video.
  • Request account assistance.

Third-Party Services

BMTheaterHub may use trusted third-party services including Cloudinary for media storage and MongoDB for secure database management.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
